Rebuild KPI 1 figures

KPI 1 is worked out once and stored, because scoring twenty thousand tickets on every page load would be too slow — so an edit to ROSTER, PERSON_MODULES, SECTION_MODULES or TICKET_MODULES in Config.gs changes nothing until the stored figures are worked out again. This is the button that does that.

Nothing is uploaded or deleted, and KPI 2 is untouched — it scores live, so a Config.gs edit shows up there straight away. Nothing here is waiting on you: this is a tool for after a settings change, not a task to finish.
